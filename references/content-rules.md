# Content rules

## Audience and voice

- Write for AI beginners.
- Explain through a compact story or concrete action.
- Be simple without becoming inaccurate.
- Lead with the point; avoid textbook definitions and jargon chains.
- Use one memorable sentence per page.

## Accuracy

- Distinguish analogy from mechanism.
- Verify “是什么、为什么、怎么用/做”.
- Clarify ambiguous or polysemous terms before drafting.
- Include a limitation when omission could create a false mental model.
- For probabilistic systems, explain that fluent output is not proof of truth.

## Text limits

- Provide one recommended post title and two alternatives.
- Keep post body under 200 Chinese characters.
- Keep in-image explanations concise enough to read on a phone.
- Avoid centered explanatory paragraphs.

## Page-copy pattern

Use this structure when appropriate:

- Cover: question or tension.
- Definition: “它就是……”
- Mechanism: “它是如何……”
- Capability: “同一种能力，可以……”
- Boundary: “……不代表……”

Do not force the pattern when another narrative is clearer.

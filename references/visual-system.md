# Approved visual system

## Canvas and layout

- Canvas: 1086 × 1448 px, 3:4 portrait.
- Background: pale cool gray studio backdrop with subtle texture and soft floor shadow.
- Cover: large centered headline, visual subject below.
- Content pages: two-line left-aligned headline at a shared top-left anchor; central illustration; cream-white bordered explanation panel near the bottom.
- Explanations never sit in the visual center.

## Typography

- Heavy geometric Chinese display face for headlines.
- Deep graphite black for primary text.
- Bright saturated orange matching the approved cover `LLM` letters for keywords.
- Use orange selectively, normally one phrase or two key words.
- All content-page headlines must share identical per-character size, weight, top margin, left margin, and line spacing. Do not size by bounding-box width.
- Corner labels are small, thin, and consistent.

## Illustration language

- Premium retro-futuristic 3D language machines.
- Silver aluminum shells, dark graphite interiors, restrained orange light paths, and sparse blue indicators.
- Soft studio lighting, realistic rounded metal, clean product-render finish.
- Machines can vary by page; consistency comes from materials, palette, lighting, and graphic system—not from repeating one character.
- Every object must explain content. Remove redundant modules, duplicate conveyors, stray dialogue cards, or decorative clutter.

## Fixed corner labels

- Top-left: `2026`
- Top-right: `ZHELI`
- Bottom-left: `AI`
- Bottom-right: variable keyword, maximum four characters

## Approved LLM example assets

- `assets/approved-llm-example/01-cover.png` is the approved cover master.
- `02-definition.png` through `05-limit.png` demonstrate the content-page system.
- Use them for palette, materials, hierarchy, explanation panels, and corner labels.
- Do not reuse their LLM-specific copy when explaining another term.
